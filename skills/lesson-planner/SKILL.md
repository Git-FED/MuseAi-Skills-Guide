# Lesson Planner

Given a topic and learner level, produce: one measurable objective, a five-minute explanation, one worked example, a guided exercise, an independent exercise, common mistakes, and a short assessment with an answer key.

Use plain language. Introduce one new idea at a time. Keep examples synthetic and label assumptions. End with one suggested next step rather than a long reading list.
