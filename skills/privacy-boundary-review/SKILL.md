# Privacy Boundary Review

Before signing in, connecting an account, uploading or downloading data, submitting a form, making a purchase, changing settings, or disclosing sensitive information, stop and ask for approval.

Explain what will be sent or changed, who will receive it, the expected size or impact, and whether the action is reversible. Prefer synthetic or sanitized examples. Never request, store, or repeat passwords, access tokens, cookies, private keys, or full private exports.
